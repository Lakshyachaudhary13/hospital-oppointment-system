# Master Defense Viva Q&A Guide (Top 10 Managerial Questions & Answers)

### Q1: *"How does CarePlus ensure high appointment attendance and minimize missed slots?"*
* **Model Managerial Answer:**  
  *"CarePlus promotes high attendance through three proactive measures: First, immediate digital confirmation with automated reminder notifications ensures appointments remain top-of-mind. Second, optional digital pre-registration creates mutual commitment. Third, an automated real-time rescheduling window allows vacated slots to be dynamically offered to waiting patients, ensuring clinical capacity is continuously maximized."*

### Q2: *"Why choose a responsive web application over a native mobile app?"*
* **Model Managerial Answer:**  
  *"From a patient accessibility perspective, web applications eliminate friction: patients can immediately book appointments on any smartphone without downloading a large app. From an institutional IT standpoint, maintaining a single unified web solution reduces maintenance, hosting, and update expenses by over 60% compared to dual-platform native apps."*

### Q3: *"How does this project apply Queuing Theory principles from Operations Management?"*
* **Model Managerial Answer:**  
  *"Traditional walk-in outpatient clinics operate as variable arrival systems where surges create long queues. CarePlus transforms this into a scheduled, deterministic distribution model. By smoothing patient arrivals evenly throughout clinical hours, service capacity matches patient arrival rates, minimizing waiting times."*

### Q4: *"How do you encourage senior medical specialists to adopt digital scheduling?"*
* **Model Managerial Answer:**  
  *"Adoption is facilitated through zero-friction design. Specialists do not need to manage complex software; administrative coordinators manage schedule availability on their behalf. In addition, transparent capacity analytics demonstrate how the platform maximizes consultation volume and enhances practice productivity."*

### Q5: *"How does CarePlus differentiate from third-party healthcare aggregator portals?"*
* **Model Managerial Answer:**  
  *"Third-party aggregator portals operate consumer directories where hospitals must compete for leads and pay per-booking commissions. CarePlus is an institutional, private-label solution. It enables hospitals to own 100% of their patient relationships, retain data sovereignty, and strengthen their direct institutional brand equity."*

### Q6: *"How does CarePlus align with national digital health standards like ABDM?"*
* **Model Managerial Answer:**  
  *"CarePlus is designed around standard healthcare interoperability principles. It utilizes standardized data schemas compatible with the Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission (ABDM) and FHIR guidelines, incorporates Role-Based Access Control, and uses TLS 1.3 cryptographic encryption for secure patient record management."*

### Q7: *"What are the primary assumptions behind the 2.8-month payback period?"*
* **Model Managerial Answer:**  
  *"The payback model is based on a standard 25-doctor outpatient facility conducting ~100 daily consultations with an average ₹500 consultation fee. By maximizing slot utilization and optimizing front-desk administrative efficiency, the facility gains over ₹2.28 Lakhs in net monthly value, recovering the initial setup investment in just 2.8 months."*

### Q8: *"How does the system ensure uninterrupted operations during network fluctuations?"*
* **Model Managerial Answer:**  
  *"The system utilizes intelligent client-side caching. The front-desk terminal retains the daily schedule manifest locally, allowing patient check-ins and triage to continue smoothly even during temporary internet interruptions, with automatic synchronization once connectivity is restored."*

### Q9: *"What is the Go-To-Market strategy for acquiring healthcare institutional clients?"*

  *"We utilize a land-and-expand approach, offering medical centers a low-risk 30-day pilot in a single department. Once department heads observe measurable reductions in reception congestion and gains in doctor utilization, the platform is seamlessly expanded hospital-wide."*
